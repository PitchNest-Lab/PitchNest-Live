# PitchNest — Landing Page Copy (v2, pain-led & honest)

**Purpose:** Rewrite the landing page around **pain → solution → why act now**, so a founder immediately feels
"this is for me." Reworked to be **honest to what's shipped today** (an AI investor panel that *listens*,
interrogates your deck, and scores your readiness) and to **remove fabricated proof** (fake testimonials with
raise amounts, fake "trusted-by" logos, invented analytics %) — which violated our own "honest over impressive"
principle.

**Positioning one-liner:** *Rehearse your raise with an AI investor panel that grills you — then get a report
that tells you exactly what to fix before the real meeting.* (The panel is the **method**; the **report** is the
payoff; "get investor-ready" is the **promise**.)

**Voice:** direct, founder-to-founder, a little tough-love. No hype, no fake numbers.

**Capability guardrails:** ❌ video/vision, eye-contact/posture/facial analysis · ❌ "pacing"/WPM (not measured —
but filler-word %, confidence, and talk ratio *are*, so lean on those) · ❌ predicting or guaranteeing funding ·
❌ any user counts, testimonials, raise figures, or "thousands of pitches analyzed" benchmarks until they're real.

## 3) The Problem  *(name the pain)*

**Section label:** The problem

**Heading:** Most founders find the gaps live — in the room, when it's already too late.

**Body:**
- You get **one shot** with each investor. Fumble the Q&A and that lead is gone.
- Rejections come back **vague** — "not a fit right now" — with zero on *why*.
- Your friends and co-founders are **too nice** to grill you the way a real partner will.
- The pitch isn't the hard part. It's the **follow-up questions you didn't see coming** — on your TAM, your
  unit economics, your moat.

**Kicker:** So you walk in under-rehearsed, get caught off guard, and burn a lead you can't get back.

## 4) The Solution  *(what PitchNest is)*

**Section label:** The solution

**Heading:** Get grilled in private, so nothing surprises you in public.

**Body:** Pitch out loud to a panel of AI investors — a lead partner, a financial analyst, and a technical
partner. They listen to your delivery, interrupt with hard questions, and debate your thesis. When you're done,
you get a **structured readiness report**: an overall score, where you're strong, your red flags, and the
specific fixes to make before your next real meeting.

**Supporting points (3):**
- **A panel that actually pushes back** — adaptive follow-up questions based on what you say, not a script.
- **It reads your deck** — upload it and the panel asks about *your* numbers: TAM, unit economics, go-to-market.
- **An honest score, not a pep talk** — readiness graded on delivery, clarity, market/scalability, and overall
  strength, with concrete fixes.

## 7) In Your Report  *(the deliverable — this is the payoff)*

**Section label:** In your report
**Heading:** Walk away with a battle plan, not just a score.

The second you finish, PitchNest generates a detailed readiness report — the kind of prep a pitch coach would
charge for:

- **Verdict + executive summary** — Invest / watch / decline, and *why*, in plain language.
- **Readiness scorecard** — Overall score with a breakdown across delivery, clarity, scalability, and readiness.
- **"If you pitched again today"** — Your projected score once you fix the flagged gaps (e.g. **46 → 78**), so you
  know exactly what's on the table.
- **Delivery analytics** — Filler-word %, talk ratio, confidence over time, and how many questions you fielded.
- **Strengths & critical risks** — What landed, and the red flags that will sink you if you don't fix them.
- **Toughest questions + answer frameworks** — The questions you fumbled, with a structure to nail them next time.
- **Competitive landscape & SWOT** — AI-identified competitors, market positioning, and gaps to exploit *(estimates labeled as such)*.
- **Prioritized action plan + practice drills** — Ranked fixes and short drills to run before your next session.
- **Progress tracking** — Scores saved across sessions so you can watch your readiness climb.
- **Shareable** — Send the read-only report to co-founders, advisors, or your accelerator.

## 10) FAQ

- **What is PitchNest?** A private rehearsal room where you pitch to an AI investor panel that questions your
  deck and scores your readiness — so you fix the gaps before real meetings.
- **How realistic is the panel?** It asks the kinds of pointed, adaptive questions real partners do and debates
  your thesis. It's practice, not a prediction of any investor's decision.
- **Can I upload my pitch deck?** Yes — the panel reads it and asks questions grounded in your slides and numbers.
- **Does it judge how I look on camera?** Not today. PitchNest analyzes your spoken pitch and deck. (Visual
  delivery analysis is on our roadmap.)
- **Is it free?** Free during early access — no card required.
- **What do I get after a session?** A detailed readiness report — verdict, scorecard, delivery analytics,
  competitive landscape/SWOT, a prioritized action plan with practice drills, and your projected score if you
  fix the gaps — plus a replay you can review and share.
- **Can I practice more than once?** As much as you want — that's the point. Your scores are saved so you can
  track improvement.

## What to REMOVE from the current page
1. **Fabricated testimonials** (Jessica Martinez "closed $8M," David Chen, etc.) — all invented.
2. **Fake "trusted by" logos** (HealthTech AI, Quantum Labs, FinFlow, DataSync, CloudNest, AI Forge).
3. **Invented analytics %** (Pitch Clarity 92% / Market Knowledge 85% / …) — not real platform data, and the
   labels don't even match the app's real scoring axes.
4. **"Delivery Feedback — pacing"** wording — the app measures filler-word %, confidence, and talk ratio, but
   **not** pace/WPM. Keep the (real) delivery analytics; just drop "pacing."
5. Any copy implying the AI **sees** the founder or **predicts/guarantees** funding.

## Honesty note — also fix in the REPORT (same principle)
The generated report currently says *"PitchNest analyzes thousands of founder pitches across Africa and
globally"* with percentile benchmarks (53rd; avg 42; top 25% 68). Pre-launch that is almost certainly
synthetic — the same fabricated-proof issue as the landing testimonials. Either compute it from real sessions
once there's volume, or soften it (e.g. "benchmarked against our scoring rubric") until it's true. The
competitor size figures are fine — they're labeled "AI estimates."
